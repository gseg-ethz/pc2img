  - **JP 5281518 B2** (machine-translated English, labeled): its claim actually includes a slope-red + openness composition, but the distinguishing limitation is mapping components into **CIE L\*a\*b\* channels** and forming a **second composite image (KLi)** for stereoscopic output — which `rrim.py` (plain `stack((red, base, base))` RGB) does not do.
  - **US 11,836,856 B2** (verbatim English): every substantive limitation is a **super-resolution / elevation-smoothing** step (divide-distance odd-number sub-mesh, interpolated fine grid-cells, smoothing meshes, consideration distances) that `rrim.py` does not perform.
- **Accepted ADR** (`03.1-ADR-rrim-ip-disposition.md`): locks D-09 (keep on expiry basis), D-10 (external wait → in-house sign-off), D-11 (opt-in `rrim` extra + NOTICE, out of default barrel), D-12 (keep name, nominative use). Cites the findings doc, records residual caveats and the A5 disposition; keeps D-NN/A-NN IDs in the planning tree where they resolve.
