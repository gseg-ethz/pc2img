# ADR: RRIM (Red Relief Image Map) IP disposition — keep on patent-expiry basis

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-07-10
**Phase:** 03.1 — rrim-red-relief-image-ip-status-clarification
**Deciders:** pc2img maintainer (ETH Zurich, GSEG)
**Evidence base:** [`docs/ip/rrim-ip-findings.md`](../../../docs/ip/rrim-ip-findings.md)
(shipped IP findings document)

> This ADR lives in the planning tree, where GSD decision IDs (D-NN) and research
> assumption IDs (A-NN) resolve. The shipped findings doc it cites
> (`docs/ip/rrim-ip-findings.md`) deliberately omits those IDs because it reaches
> `main`, where `.planning/` is stripped.

## Context

`src/pc2img/features/rrim.py` implements the red-relief-image visualization
technique (slope-reddening + positive/negative topographic openness →
brightness, composed into a flat 2-D RGB raster). The technique is associated
with **Asia Air Survey Co., Ltd. (AAS)** / inventor **Tatsuro Chiba**, and
`rrim.py` had never been pushed to the public remote — so its first public push
would be its first public exposure. This created a blocking IP/publication gate
(the todo `2026-07-09-rrim-patent-review-before-public.md`) that also blocks
Phase 3's live-CI UAT (resolve-then-push, per D-07).

The disposition was **findings-driven, not pre-committed** (D-02, now
discharged): the CONTEXT decision tree offered keep-behind-gate (D-03), separate
package (D-04), or strip-from-history (D-05), to be selected only after
investigating patents, jurisdictions, claims, expiry, and license availability.

Research (`03.1-RESEARCH.md`) established the decisive fact: the **core RRIM
patent family — the "Visualizing system, method, and program" invention that
actually claims the composition `rrim.py` implements — has EXPIRED in every
jurisdiction** (JP 3670274, CN 1711568B, TW I285852, and US 7,764,282 B2, whose
adjusted expiration 2025-10-05 is already past). Expiry places the claimed
invention in the public domain, so redistribution needs **no AAS license** — a
stronger and simpler basis than the research/educational license the original
D-03 premise assumed.

## Decision

Adopt the locked disposition resolved post-research (D-09 … D-12):

### D-09 — Keep `rrim.py` on a patent-expiry basis (= D-03)

Keep `rrim.py` in the distribution. The redistribution basis is
**public-domain-on-expiry** of the core patent family, evidenced in
`docs/ip/rrim-ip-findings.md`. **No license acquisition** is required.
D-04 (relocate to a separate package) and **D-05 (strip from tree + history)**
are **NOT executed** — this commits D-02's open choice to D-03. `rrim.py` reads
on the now-expired core claim but, on a good-faith non-binding read, not on the
two still-active AAS patents.

### D-10 — External license-wait collapses to internal good-faith sign-off

Because expiry removes the AAS-license dependency, the D-07 ETH tech-transfer
step downgrades from a **blocking external license-wait** to a **non-blocking,
good-faith ETH/owner sign-off documented in-repo** (delivered in Plan 03). No
external dependency blocks the phase; the settled branch is publication-safe once
the disposition executes, so Phase 3's live-CI push proceeds without an external
wait. This refines D-07/D-08.

### D-11 — Expose RRIM via an opt-in `rrim` extra carrying the NOTICE

RRIM stays loadable via an explicit opt-in `rrim` extra in `pyproject.toml`
(matching the existing cuda11/cuda12 extras pattern) that carries the attribution
NOTICE. The module is **kept OUT of the default public feature barrel**
(`src/pc2img/features/__init__.py`) — registration remains import-time only. The
`rrim.py` docstring's advertised `generate(["rrim", ...])` API is reconciled to
document the opt-in path (delivered in Plan 02). Per D-06, the extra gates only
optional **dependencies**, not the `.py` source (which ships in the wheel
regardless); the actual clearance is the legal NOTICE + expiry basis, not the
packaging.

### D-12 — Keep the "RRIM" / "Red Relief Image Map" name (nominative use)

Keep the name under **nominative/descriptive use** with clear AAS trademark
(RRIM®) attribution in the NOTICE. **No rename** of the feature or its DSL key.
Trademark is a distinct right unaffected by patent expiry; the mitigation is
attribution, not removal.

## Consequences

**Positive**
- The blocking IP gate clears on a citable, durable fact (expired patents do not
  un-expire); Phase 3's live-CI UAT is unblocked with a single clean push.
- No AAS contact or license negotiation is required for the base technique.
- The suite stays green and coverage unchanged — no strip/relocate churn (D-05's
  history rewrite and coverage rebaseline are avoided).

**Negative / residual caveats**
- **Two still-active AAS patents** remain (JP 5281518, stereoscopic, JP-only, to
  2029; US 11,836,856, super-resolution stereoscopic, US-only, to 2040). The
  findings doc's good-faith read concludes `rrim.py` does not read on either, but
  the read is **explicitly non-binding** (assumptions A1–A5) — an engineering
  opinion, not legal advice.
- **Trademark is unaffected by patent expiry.** The "RRIM®" mark survives; the
  nominative-use position (A3) is defensible but not certified.
- The good-faith infringement/redistribution read (A1, A2, A4) is what the
  in-house ETH/owner sign-off (D-10, Plan 03) exists to acknowledge.

## A5 disposition — verbatim independent-claim text (RESEARCH Open-Question Q3)

Research assumption **A5** flagged that the non-infringement read must walk the
**actual independent-claim text** of the two active patents rather than a
paraphrase (RESEARCH.md Open-Question Q3). Disposition: `docs/ip/rrim-ip-findings.md`
**quotes the verbatim independent-claim text** for both active patents where
sourceable — US 11,836,856 B2 claim 1 verbatim (Google Patents English), and
JP 5281518 B2 claim 1 in Google Patents' machine-translated English (labeled as
such, the authoritative text being the original Japanese). Both were sourced at
execution time, so **no accepted-residual paraphrase fallback was needed**. This
ADR is the planning-tree home for the A5 traceability; the shipped findings doc
omits the "A5" ID by design (it would dangle once `.planning/` is stripped on
`main`).

## Alternatives not chosen

- **D-04 (separate optional package):** unnecessary — expiry means there is no
  scope-limited license whose redistribution reach needs isolating.
- **D-05 (strip from tree + history):** retained in RESEARCH.md as the fully
  specced fallback should ETH tech-transfer dispute the expiry read, but **not
  executed**; the expiry basis is HIGH-confidence and citable.
